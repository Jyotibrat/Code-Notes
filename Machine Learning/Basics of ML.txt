Training and Testing Data:

Training Data: A subset of data to train a model.

Testing Data: A subset of data test the trained model.

Make sure that Test Dataset meets the following 2 conditions:

1) Large enough to yield statistically meaningful results.
2) Representative of the data set as a whole.
3) In other words, do not pick a test dataset with different characteristics than the training dataset.
